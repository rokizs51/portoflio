This page has one job: keep an honest log of what I'm learning, as I'm learning it.

I've found that the things I understand best are the things I was forced to write
down. Half-understood ideas survive contact with a blank text editor about as well
as a house of cards survives a fan — so the notes here stay close to the mess:
wrong turns included, dead ends labeled.

## What to expect

- **Short posts, written while the problem is still warm.** Notes from whatever
  I'm building or breaking that week.
- **Corrections in place, not as gotchas.** When I learn I was wrong, I update
  the post and say so — the `updated on` date tells you when.
- **No listicle padding.** If I can't say something specific and true, it doesn't
  get published.
